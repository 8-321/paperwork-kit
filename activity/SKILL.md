# KDP Activity Book Builder

Open `index.html`, set the title, theme, counts and seed, then Build and Print → Save as PDF.

- **Mazes**: 10 × 12 cells, START top-left, FINISH bottom-right; solutions at the back.
- **Word searches**: 12 × 12 grid, 8 theme words, across/down/diagonal; answer page underlines the found words.
- **Sudoku**: curated puzzles with verified unique solutions, easy (40 clues) or medium (32 clues); solutions at the back.
- **Cover**: title, subtitle, "this book belongs to" box.
- The seed controls mazes, letter placement and puzzle selection. Same seed, same book.

Print at 100% on US Letter. For KDP interiors keep at least 0.5 in margins, no bleed for this layout, and an even page count.

No network, no account, no AI model.
