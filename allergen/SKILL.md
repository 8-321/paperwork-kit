# Allergen Matrix Maker

Open `index.html`, enter the business name and dishes, and tick every allergen each dish contains.

- Choose **UK/EU 14** or **US 9**; the grid and legend rebuild.
- A blank cell means "not used in this recipe". Verify with the kitchen; do not guess.
- The print sheet has vertical allergen headers, a legend and a "contains, by dish" summary.
- CSV export: one row per dish, one column per allergen, `1` = contains, `0` = does not.
- Set the review date every time the menu changes.

This is a documentation tool, not legal or medical advice. No network, no account, no AI model.
